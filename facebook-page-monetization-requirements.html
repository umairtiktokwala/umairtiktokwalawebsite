Facebook Page monetization requirements — the full checklist

Exactly what a Page needs before Meta lets it earn — and the criteria that quietly block most Pages even after the numbers are met.

By Muhammad Umair · Umair TikTok Wala Academy · 17,000+ students since 2019 · Updated September 2026

Short answer: 10,000 followers + 150,000 views in the last 30 days + original content + clean Page Quality + age 18+ + eligible country + a payout account in your own name. Meet all seven and the Content Monetization option appears in Professional Dashboard → Monetization.

The 7 criteria

  • A Page (or professional-mode profile) you admin. Personal profiles in normal mode cannot monetize.
  • 10,000 followers. Followers, not likes — the two are different numbers on a Page.
  • 150,000 views in the last 30 days. A rolling window; one viral Reel can push you over, a quiet month can drop you under.
  • Original content. Made by you. Reposts from TikTok/YouTube/other Pages, stock-clip compilations and CapCut templates count as "reused" or "unoriginal" and block eligibility.
  • Page Quality with no violations. Check Professional Dashboard → Page Quality. Any "Limited" or "Restricted" status for monetization, copyright or community standards blocks you until it clears.
  • Age 18+ and an eligible country. Pakistan is eligible.
  • Payout and tax details. Bank account in the Page owner's exact name; tax form completed. Missing or mismatched details stop payments even after approval.

The criteria that fail Pages quietly

  • Reused content. The single biggest reason. Even 3–4 reposted videos can mark a Page "not eligible for monetization".
  • Engagement bait. "Comment 1 for part 2", "Share for luck" posts trigger a Page Quality flag.
  • Copyrighted music. Songs not from Facebook's library get videos muted or blocked from earning.
  • Misleading titles / thumbnails. Clickbait flags the Page.
  • Buying followers or views. Inauthentic behaviour = permanent ineligibility.
  • Several Pages from one phone with multi-space apps. Linked-account flags.

How to check your Page right now

  1. Open the Page → Professional Dashboard.
  2. Tap Monetization — it lists each requirement with a tick or the number you still need.
  3. Tap Page Quality — it must say "No violations". If not, follow the appeal link there before doing anything else.

Fastest way to meet the numbers

  • One niche, one audience, daily Reels of 30–90 seconds — most Pages cross 10k/150k in 30–60 days this way.
  • AI-generated video (Google Flow / Veo, Whisk, CapCut) is original if you made it — and lets you post daily without a camera.
  • Target English-speaking viewers if you want meaningful earnings; Pakistani views help you qualify but pay very little.

Frequently asked questions

How many followers do I need to monetize a Facebook Page?

10,000 followers, plus 150,000 views in the last 30 days, original content and a clean Page Quality.

Do Page likes count as followers?

No. Followers and likes are separate numbers. Monetization uses followers.

I meet the numbers but I am not eligible — why?

Usually Page Quality: reused content, engagement bait, copyrighted music or a violation. Check Professional Dashboard → Page Quality and fix that first.

Are the requirements the same in Pakistan?

Yes. Pakistan is an eligible country and the criteria are the same; payouts go to a Pakistani bank.

Does AI-generated content count as original?

Yes, if you created it. Template videos and stock-clip compilations do not.
