Facebook monetization eligible countries — 2026 list

Facebook pays creators in far more countries than TikTok does — Pakistan included. Here is the list, the exceptions, and the one thing that matters more than your country.

By Muhammad Umair · Umair TikTok Wala Academy · 17,000+ students since 2019 · Updated September 2026

Short answer: Facebook Content Monetization is available in 150+ countries, including Pakistan, India, Bangladesh, Saudi Arabia, UAE, Egypt, Nigeria, Kenya, Indonesia and the Philippines. Your Page's country decides if you can join; your audience's country decides how much you earn.

Eligible — by region (2026)

RegionEligible countries (examples)Note

Meta expands and occasionally pauses countries; the authoritative check is inside your Page: Professional Dashboard → Monetization. If your country is not supported it says so there.

Not eligible / restricted

  • Countries under sanctions (e.g. Iran, North Korea, Syria, Cuba) — no monetization.
  • Some smaller markets are "invite-only" for Content Monetization even if in-stream ads were available before.
  • Facebook itself is restricted in a few countries (e.g. China) — check locally.

Why your audience's country matters more

A Pakistani Page with a Pakistani audience can be fully monetized and still earn a few dollars a month, because advertisers pay very little for Pakistani views. The same Page with US/UK viewers earns many times more. That is why the real question is not "is my country eligible?" (it is) but "who watches my content?" — and why we teach English, AI-made, global-niche content from day one.

Pakistan specifically

  • Eligible for Content Monetization, in-stream ads and Stars.
  • Payouts to Pakistani banks in the Page owner's name; tax form required; non-filers face higher withholding.
  • Full guide: Facebook monetization in Pakistan · Checklist: requirements.

TikTok is the opposite

TikTok's Creator Rewards Program is open in only a handful of countries — and Pakistan is not one of them. If you also want TikTok income, read TikTok monetization countries.

Frequently asked questions

Is Facebook monetization available in Pakistan?

Yes. Pakistan is an eligible country for Content Monetization, in-stream ads and Stars, with payouts to Pakistani banks.

Which countries are eligible for Facebook monetization?

150+ countries including Pakistan, India, Bangladesh, Saudi Arabia, UAE, Egypt, Nigeria, Kenya, Indonesia, the Philippines, the US, UK and most of Europe and Latin America.

Is Facebook monetization available in India?

Yes, India is eligible for Content Monetization; earnings depend on audience region.

Do I need a VPN to monetize Facebook in Pakistan?

No. Facebook monetizes Pakistani Pages directly. A VPN is only relevant for a separate foreign-audience setup, and mixing locations on one account is risky.

Why does my monetized Page earn so little?

Because your viewers are in a low-RPM country. Earnings follow the audience, not the Page owner. Target English-speaking viewers to earn more.
