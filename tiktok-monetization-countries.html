TikTok monetization countries — who is eligible in 2026

The Creator Rewards Program is only open in some countries. Here is the current list, the countries that are not on it, and the route creators in non-eligible countries use.

By Muhammad Umair · Umair TikTok Wala Academy · 17,000+ students since 2019 · Updated September 2026

Short answer: Creator Rewards is available in the United States, United Kingdom, Germany, France, Japan, South Korea and Brazil, with a few more markets added over time. Pakistan, India, Bangladesh, Nepal, Sri Lanka, most of Africa and most of the Middle East are not eligible. Other TikTok programs (LIVE gifts, Shop, brand deals) have their own, separate country lists.

  • Middle East: Saudi Arabia, UAE, Qatar, Egypt — LIVE gifts available, Creator Rewards not
  • Africa: Nigeria, Kenya, South Africa, Ghana — not eligible

If your country is not eligible — what creators actually do

  1. Run an account set up in an eligible region — usually the US or UK. Created and used with one fixed location (dedicated proxy or paid VPN), separate device or browser profile, new Gmail. This is the standard route for Pakistani creators; read the Pakistan guide.
  2. Make content for that region's audience — English, global topics (nature, mini dramas, animals, transformations). Local-language content will not get the views.
  3. Meet the same requirements — 10k followers, 100k views, 60-second originals. Checklist.
  4. Solve payouts — US accounts pay via PayPal, which many non-eligible countries (including Pakistan) do not have. Creators use a trusted PayPal Service that pays them locally, or a relative abroad.

Warning: the account's region must never change once created. Verifying a US/UK account with a Pakistani ID, switching VPN countries, or buying a "monetized account" are the three fastest ways to lose everything.

Frequently asked questions

Is TikTok monetization available in Pakistan?

No. Pakistan is not an eligible country for the Creator Rewards Program. Pakistani creators run accounts set up in the US or UK.

Which countries have TikTok Creator Rewards?

United States, United Kingdom, Germany, France, Japan, South Korea and Brazil, with more markets added over time. Check TikTok Studio for your account.

Is TikTok monetization available in Saudi Arabia or UAE?

LIVE gifts are available there, but the Creator Rewards Program is not, as of 2026.

Can I change my TikTok account region to an eligible country?

Not safely on an existing account. Creators create a new account with a fixed eligible-region location from the start.

How do creators in non-eligible countries get paid?

Through a US or UK account paid via PayPal, then withdrawn through a PayPal service or a relative abroad.
