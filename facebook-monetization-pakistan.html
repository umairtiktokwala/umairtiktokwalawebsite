Facebook monetization in Pakistan — requirements, payouts and what to expect

Good news first: unlike TikTok, Facebook pays creators in Pakistan directly. The bad news is that Pakistani views pay very little. This guide covers both — how to get monetized, how the money reaches your bank, and how to earn more than a few cents.

By Muhammad Umair · Umair TikTok Wala Academy · 17,000+ students since 2019 · Updated September 2026

Short answer: Yes — Facebook's Content Monetization program is available in Pakistan. It is one program that pays for Reels, videos, photos, text posts and Stories. You need a Facebook Page (not a personal profile), the follower and view thresholds below, original content, and a payout account in your own name.

How Facebook pays in Pakistan: Content Monetization

Facebook has merged its older programs (in-stream ads, Ads on Reels, Performance bonus) into one program — Content Monetization. Once your Page is accepted, you earn from every content type in one place:

Content typeHow you earn
ReelsShort vertical videos — the biggest earner for most Pages
VideosLonger videos, including videos over 1 minute
PhotosSingle photos and photo posts
Text postsWritten posts on the Page
StoriesPage Stories

Earnings depend on how your content performs — views, engagement and where your audience is. This guide focuses on Content Monetization, because that is the program Pakistani Pages get today.

Requirements to get a Page monetized

  1. A Facebook Page in professional mode — not a personal profile only.
  2. 10,000 followers on the Page.
  3. 150,000 views on your content in the last 30 days.
  4. Original content — no reposts, no copied videos, no reused clips from other creators.
  5. No policy violations — check Page Quality; a single "limited" strike blocks monetization.
  6. Age 18+ and a Pakistani bank account or payout method in your own name.

The catch nobody tells beginners: monetization depends on where your viewers are, not where you are. Pakistani views pay roughly 10–20× less than US or UK views. A Page with 1 million Pakistani views can earn under $10; the same views from the US can earn a few hundred dollars. Content language and topic decide who watches — which is why most of our students build English-language, global-audience Pages using AI video.

How to get monetized — 6 steps

  1. Create a Page, not just a profile. Set the category, add a profile picture, cover and a short about section. Turn on professional mode. One device, one account.
  2. Choose one niche and one audience. Nature, mini dramas, animals, transformations, mini worlds — in English if you want US/UK views. Do not mix niches on one Page.
  3. Post original content daily. Reels of 30–90 seconds are the core; add videos, photos and text posts too — Content Monetization pays on all of them. AI-generated content counts as original if you made it.
  4. Reach the threshold. 10,000 followers and 150,000 views in 30 days — usually 30–60 days of daily posting. Watch the Professional Dashboard.
  5. Apply and set up payouts. When the monetization tool appears, apply, then add your bank details and tax information. Name on the bank account must match the Page owner's ID.
  6. Get paid monthly. Facebook pays around the 21st of each month once you cross the payout minimum. Money lands in your Pakistani bank in 3–5 working days.

Payouts in Pakistan: bank, tax and common problems

  • Bank — most students use Meezan, UBL, HBL or Bank Alfalah. Add the account in Payout Settings with the exact name on your CNIC.
  • Tax — Facebook asks for tax information (W-8BEN for non-US creators). Fill it correctly; a blocked tax form stops the next payout.
  • Withholding in Pakistan — non-filers lose around 20% at the bank; registering as an FBR filer reduces it.
  • Minimum payout — earnings below the minimum roll over to the next month.
  • "Payout not received" — check three things: status shows Paid, 3–5 working days have passed, bank details match your ID.

Mistakes that get Pages demonetized

  • Reposting videos from TikTok, YouTube or other Pages (reused content)
  • Buying followers or views
  • Running several Pages from one phone with "multi-space" apps
  • Payout account in someone else's name
  • Clickbait titles and misleading thumbnails (Page Quality strike)
  • Switching niches every week

How much can you earn?

Honest answer: it depends almost entirely on audience region and views. Pakistani-audience Pages often earn $5–$50 a month even with millions of views. Global or US/UK-audience Pages can earn a few hundred to a few thousand dollars a month with the same effort — which is why we teach English, AI-made content from day one. Nobody can promise a number; anyone who does is selling something.

Frequently asked questions

Is Facebook monetization available in Pakistan?

Yes. The Content Monetization program is available to Pakistani Pages that meet the requirements — it pays for Reels, videos, photos, text posts and Stories. Payouts go to a Pakistani bank account.

Do I need a VPN for Facebook monetization in Pakistan?

No. Unlike TikTok, Facebook monetizes Pakistani Pages directly. A VPN is only relevant if you are targeting a foreign audience with a separate setup — and mixing locations on one account is risky.

Why is my monetized Page earning only $0.01?

Because your viewers are in Pakistan. RPM (earnings per 1,000 views) for Pakistani audiences is very low. Change the content language and topic to reach US/UK viewers.

Does AI-generated video count as original?

Yes, if you generated it and it follows Meta's guidelines. Template videos and stock clips are treated as reused content.

The monetization tool hasn't appeared even though I meet the criteria.

Meta can take 1–3 weeks after you cross the threshold. Check Page Quality for violations, Page country and settings, then wait.
