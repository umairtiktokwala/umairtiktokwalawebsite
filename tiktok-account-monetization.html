How to monetize a TikTok account — from zero to first payout

Not the requirements list — the actual order of work. This is the process our students follow, in the order that keeps the account safe.

By Muhammad Umair · Umair TikTok Wala Academy · 17,000+ students since 2019 · Updated September 2026

In one line: set the account up right → warm it up → one niche, original videos over 60 seconds, daily → cross 10k followers and 100k monthly views → apply → verify identity and tax → add payout → get paid monthly.

The 8 steps

  1. Decide the region before you create the account. Creator Rewards only pays in eligible regions (US, UK and a few more). If you live there, use your normal phone. If you are in Pakistan or another non-eligible country, set up a fixed US/UK location first — dedicated proxy or paid VPN, separate device or browser profile, new Gmail. Never mix locations later.
  2. Create the account properly. Personal account, real-looking profile (photo, short bio, one niche), no links to other accounts yet. Do not import contacts.
  3. Warm it up for 5–7 days. Only watch, like, follow and comment inside your niche and region. No posting. This teaches TikTok what your account is and where it lives; accounts that post on day one get almost no reach.
  4. Pick one niche and stay in it. Nature, mini dramas, animals, transformations, mini worlds, mini vlogs — one only. Switching niches resets your reach.
  5. Post original videos over 60 seconds, daily. 1–2 a day for the first week, then 3–5. AI-generated video (Google Flow / Veo, Whisk, CapCut) counts as original if you made it. No reposts, no templates, no stock clips.
  6. Cross the threshold: 10,000 followers and 100,000 views in 30 days. Usually 30–60 days of consistent posting. Track it in TikTok Studio. Full list: requirements checklist.
  7. Apply for Creator Rewards and verify. TikTok Studio → Creator Rewards Program → Apply. Complete identity verification and tax information (W-8BEN outside the US). Use documents that match the account region — never a Pakistani ID on a US/UK account.
  8. Link a payout method and wait for the cycle. US accounts pay through PayPal; UK accounts can use bank in some cases. Earnings settle monthly. In Pakistan, PayPal money is withdrawn through a PayPal Service that pays you in PKR.

What to post — the content rules that keep you monetized

  • Over 60 seconds, but only as long as the story needs — watch time matters more than length.
  • Hook in the first 2 seconds; no long intros.
  • Original audio or platform library music — copyrighted songs can block earnings on that video.
  • No clickbait, no shock content, no politics — these get "not eligible for recommendation" flags.
  • Captions in the audience's language — English for US/UK.

Mistakes that disqualify an account

  • Changing location (VPN off at night, switching countries)
  • Verifying with an ID from a different country than the account
  • Buying followers, views or a "monetized account"
  • Reposting from Instagram, YouTube or other TikTok accounts
  • Running many accounts on one phone; linking one YouTube channel to several TikTok accounts
  • Posting 10+ videos on a brand-new account

How long it takes

Realistically 60–90 days to reach the threshold and 30 more days to the first payout. Students who post daily in one niche get there; students who post for two weeks and change niche do not. There is no shortcut that keeps the account safe.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to monetize a TikTok account?

Most creators need 60–90 days of daily posting in one niche to reach 10,000 followers and 100,000 monthly views, then about a month to the first payout.

Can I monetize a new TikTok account?

Yes, but warm it up for 5–7 days before posting and grow it naturally. New accounts that post immediately or buy followers get almost no reach.

Do I need a laptop to monetize TikTok?

No. A phone is enough for TikTok itself. AI video tools are easier on a laptop but not required.

Can I monetize a TikTok account from Pakistan?

Not with a Pakistani account. You need an account set up in an eligible region such as the US or UK with a consistent location, and a way to receive PayPal.

Does AI video count for TikTok monetization?

Yes, if you generated it yourself and it follows TikTok guidelines. Copied or template content does not.
