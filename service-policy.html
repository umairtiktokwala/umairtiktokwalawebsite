Creator Services Policy

Last updated: 3 August 2026

This Service Policy explains how our optional PayPal, Facebook, TikTok and related creator-support services operate. It should be read with our Terms, Privacy Policy and Refund Policy.

Services currently offered

  • Facebook Payout Setup
  • TikTok Payout Setup
  • TikTok Identity Verification guidance
  • TikTok Tax Verification guidance
  • PayPal Withdrawal Service

Availability and exact scope may change. The written offer, invoice or service conversation confirms what is included for a particular customer.

Access model

Course enrollment and service enrollment are separate. Service dashboard access is activated only after the service record has been verified and enabled. A service-only client does not automatically receive course access, and a course student does not automatically receive PayPal service access.

Customer responsibilities

  • Provide accurate, current and lawful information.
  • Use accounts and documents you are authorised to use.
  • Review names, dates, tax information, payment details and other entries before submission.
  • Comply with PayPal, Meta, TikTok, banks, wallets and applicable laws.
  • Protect passwords, OTPs, recovery codes and device access.
  • Report suspected compromise, unlinking or limitation promptly.

What we will not request

We do not need your card PIN, card security code or online-banking password. Do not submit those details through the website, app, proof upload or chat.

Third-party decisions

Platform approval, verification, payout release, account limitations, exchange rates, transaction fees and processing times are controlled by third parties. We may guide, prepare or troubleshoot, but cannot override those providers or guarantee their decisions.

PayPal assignments and settlements

Where the service includes an assigned PayPal account or managed withdrawal workflow, the authorised dashboard may show account status, assignment details, payment-method information, verified amounts, pending amounts, settlement history and service notifications.

  • Only records associated with the authenticated user should be visible to that user.
  • Payment methods must belong to or be lawfully authorised by the customer.
  • Method changes may be subject to a security cooling period before payment.
  • Settlement calculations may reflect the rate, commission, provider fee or arrangement confirmed for the service.

Verification, delays and corrections

We may request additional information, pause processing, reject unsafe instructions or correct records where information is incomplete, inconsistent or potentially fraudulent. Estimated timings are not guarantees.

Account limitations and unlinking

If a third-party account is limited, restored, changed or unlinked, the customer must notify us promptly. We may update the service status, replace an assignment where available, pause settlements or request additional verification.

No income guarantee

Support services do not guarantee monetisation, platform approval, account longevity, payout availability, revenue, audience growth or profit.
