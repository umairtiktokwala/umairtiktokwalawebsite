Facebook Content Monetization — the one program that now pays for everything

Meta merged in-stream ads, Ads on Reels and the Performance bonus into a single program. Here is how Content Monetization works, what it needs, and how to join.

By Muhammad Umair · Umair TikTok Wala Academy · 17,000+ students since 2019 · Updated September 2026

Short answer: Content Monetization is Facebook's single creator program. Once your Page is accepted, you earn from Reels, videos, photos, text posts and Stories in one place, with one dashboard and one payout. It replaced the separate in-stream ads and Reels ads programs.

Earnings are performance-based: how many people see your content, how long they engage, and — most importantly — where they are. The same views from the US or UK pay many times more than views from Pakistan or India.

Requirements to join

  • A Facebook Page (or a professional-mode profile) that you admin.
  • 10,000 followers.
  • 150,000 views on your content in the last 30 days.
  • Original content — created by you; no reposts, reused clips or template videos.
  • No monetization policy violations — clean Page Quality.
  • Age 18+, and located in an eligible country (Pakistan is eligible).

Full checklist with the fine print: Facebook Page monetization requirements.

How to join — from Monetization Manager

  1. Open your Page's Professional Dashboard → Monetization (or Meta's Monetization Manager on desktop).
  2. Check eligibility. Each requirement shows a tick or the number still needed. If you see "Content Monetization — Apply" you are eligible.
  3. Apply. Accept the Partner Monetization Policies and Content Monetization Policies.
  4. Set up payouts. Add your bank account (name exactly as on your ID) and tax information.
  5. Wait for approval. Usually a few days; up to 1–3 weeks. Keep posting during review.

Not seeing the option? Either you are below the threshold, your Page has a Page Quality issue, or the program has not reached your Page yet — Meta rolls it out gradually. Fix any violation first, keep posting, and check Monetization weekly.

How much it pays

There is no fixed rate. Pakistani-audience Pages often see a few dollars per million views; US/UK-audience Pages can see hundreds for the same views. This is why our students build English, global-audience Pages with AI-made content from day one. Anyone promising a number is selling something.

Payouts

  • Monthly, around the 21st, once you pass the payout minimum (earnings below it roll over).
  • Bank account must be in the Page owner's name; Pakistani banks (Meezan, UBL, HBL, Alfalah) all work.
  • Tax information is required; non-filers in Pakistan face higher bank withholding — register with FBR.

Frequently asked questions

What is Facebook Content Monetization?

A single creator program that pays for Reels, videos, photos, text posts and Stories on a Facebook Page. It replaced separate in-stream ads and Reels ads programs.

What are the Content Monetization requirements?

10,000 followers, 150,000 views in the last 30 days, original content, a clean Page with no policy violations, and age 18+ in an eligible country.

Is Facebook Content Monetization available in Pakistan?

Yes. Pakistani Pages that meet the requirements can join and are paid to a Pakistani bank account.

How do I apply for Content Monetization?

Professional Dashboard → Monetization → check eligibility → Apply → accept policies → add payout and tax details.

Does Content Monetization pay for photos and text posts?

Yes — that is the main change. Reels, videos, photos, text posts and Stories all earn under one program.
